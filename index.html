The Science

Here's Why, and It's Not Your Fault

Traumatic job loss keeps your brain's threat-detection system on. Cortisol affects the prefrontal cortex, the part that helps you think clearly and tell real danger from imagined danger.

"You are not broken. You are a person whose system has been running on high alert. Learning to read your signals again is coming home to yourself."

Beliefs That Feel Like Wisdom, But Aren't

❌ Sounds like wisdom

"If I feel this strongly, it must be true."

✓ What's actually true

Strong feelings are data, not always accurate data. Intensity is not the same as truth.

❌ Sounds like wisdom

"If I rest, I'm falling behind."

✓ What's actually true

Your nervous system cannot sustain a job search without recovery. Rest is how you stay in the game.

❌ Sounds like wisdom

"My hesitation means I'm too picky or too scared."

✓ What's actually true

Hesitation is information. It might be anxiety, or intuition protecting you.

Meet the Signals

Fear, Anxiety, and Intuition

⚠ Fear

A response to real, immediate physical danger. Life-saving, not a problem to manage.

  • Present-focused, happening right now
  • Full-body adrenaline response
Response: Immediate action. Listen to it.
🌀 Anxiety

Future-focused. Lives in the what-ifs and loops that never resolve.

  • Speaks in questions: "What if I fail?"
  • Repetitive and circular
Response: Compassion + truth-checking.
✦ Intuition

Your body's wisdom. Often comes back online before anything else after trauma.

  • Speaks in clear statements, not questions
  • Calm, brief, specific
Response: Trust it.
Key Distinction

Questions vs. Statements

Anxiety asks questions. Intuition makes statements.
Anxiety says: "What if I say no and they think I'm difficult?"
Intuition says: "This doesn't feel right."
One loops. The other lands.

Nuance: sometimes anxiety sounds like a statement. "I'm going to fail." Check: does your body feel calm and clear when you say it? If not, that's anxiety.

Practice

Which Signal Is This?

"What if I say no to this unpaid project and they never consider me again?"
That's anxiety. The "what if" is future-focused and circular. Truth-check: is this actually likely?
"This role isn't aligned with where I'm trying to go."
That's intuition. Calm and specific, no loop, no spiral.
"I'm going to mess this up. I always do."
This is the nuance. It sounds like a statement, but it's anxiety in disguise. True intuition doesn't attack or generalize.
"What if taking this weekend off means I fall behind?"
Anxiety again. "What if" plus worst case plus urgency. Rest is productive.
Integration

Wise Mind

DBT names three states: Emotion Mind (reactive, all-or-nothing), Reasonable Mind (rational but cold), and Wise Mind, the integration of both, calm and clear.

Emotion Mind: "I have to take this offer. What if nothing else comes?"

Reasonable Mind: "The salary is in range. Objectively, this is fine."

Wise Mind: "The numbers work. Something still feels off. I'll ask for more time."

Wise Mind is easiest to access when your nervous system is inside your window of tolerance. That's why regulation work is the foundation of decision-making.

Framework

The Three-Question Pause

1
Am I in immediate physical danger right now?

If yes: Fear. Act immediately.

2
Am I asking questions or making statements?

Questions: Anxiety. Respond with compassion.

3
Does this signal create energy or drain it?

Creates clarity: Intuition. Trust it.

"When you can tell fear, anxiety, and intuition apart, you can stop overriding your wisdom."

What Signal Is This?

Scenario 1
"I got an offer. Something keeps nagging at me. What if I say no and regret it? I can't stop going over it."
Circular "what ifs" are anxiety's signature. Truth-check: what do I actually know about this role?
Scenario 2
"Something feels off about this person. I feel physically uncomfortable. I want to leave."
Could be fear or intuition, both deserve attention. Either way: listen to it.
Scenario 3
"I keep pushing to apply even though I'm exhausted. If I stop, I'm not trying hard enough."
This is anxiety, the inner "pusher." Your exhaustion is the actual intuition. Rest is not laziness.
Scenario 4
"A recruiter asks for unpaid trial work. Something in me is already clear: this doesn't work for me."
Intuition. Clear, calm, immediate, no loop. Your body knew before your mind finished processing.
